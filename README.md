Question 1:
What is the average Math SAT score of all students in NYC?
Output: 413 (rounded)
The math SAT scores are already given as an average of each school in the dataset, 
so taking all of those scores and averaging them would give us the average of all 
of the schools.

Question 2: 
How many schools have more than 100 but less than 200 students taking the SAT?
Output: 50
This dataset includes the number of students that take the SAT in each school as a 
quantitative value, so this dataset can answer whether or not a school will have 
a number in the 100-200 range of students taking the test.

Question 3:
On average, do NYC students have higher scores in the Critical Reading or Math section of the SAT?
Output: The average Critical Reading score is higher.
Similar to the first question, the Critical Reading average scores of each school 
are also given, so we can take the average of this and compare it to the average 
of the Math scores.
